How to play

Moving

W A S D or the arrow keys walk. Hold Shift to run. Drag the mouse to look round; Q and R turn the camera; the wheel zooms.

Put your left thumb anywhere on the left half and push: a little walks, to the edge runs. Drag on the right half to look round.

Places and people

Teal rings on the ground are people and places: Tunde, Brother Sunday, Chi, Captain, Mummy, the estate office, home. Stand in a ring and press EUse to talk; pick an answer with 1 2 3a tap. The street panel lists every job and shows you the way.

Finding your way

The gold marker and the gold dot on the map show where to go next. The map turns with the camera, so up is always straight ahead.

Jobs

Jobs pay cash. Guards see in a cone on the ground: amber means they are noticing you, red means they have. Break their line of sight or run to lose them. Quiet jobs pay a bonus; shooting raises street heat. If you go down, the job starts again.

Space or F fires, once Tunde gives you a pistol after your first job.

Hold Fire to shoot, once Tunde gives you a pistol after your first job.

Money

Jobs pay cash. Deposit it into the trading app on your phone, then trade: long if you think a price will rise, short if it will fall. Leverage multiplies wins and losses. Cash on you can be taken in an arrest, so bank it. Save up to buy Mummy's house back.

Online

When the game is online, other players walk the same city: you see them in your area with their names over their heads. The online chip at the top shows who is in the city and the richest players, and lets you chat. T chats, G waves.Tap the chip to chat or wave. Everyone's trades show in the street talk.
